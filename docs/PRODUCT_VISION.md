# 1. EXECUTIVE VISION

KAOS Trading will be an all-in-one trading technology ecosystem designed to give everyday traders access to powerful trading automation, market intelligence, strategy research, advanced analytical tools, and artificial intelligence.

The goal is to create a platform where users can research markets, discover trading opportunities, connect their preferred brokers, automate trading strategies, monitor their investments, receive market alerts, and manage their portfolios—all from one KAOS account.

KAOS is not intended to be another basic trading bot or a website that simply displays trading indicators.

It will combine the capabilities of trading terminals, market scanners, automated trading systems, strategy development tools, portfolio management applications, social intelligence platforms, and AI research systems.

**The ultimate goal is to become a comprehensive trading platform that puts advanced trading technology in the hands of individual traders.**

KAOS should be designed for both beginners and experienced traders, with accessible basic features and increasingly advanced capabilities available through membership upgrades.

The platform should support automated trading, including unattended operation, but it must never guarantee profits or misrepresent investment risk.

# 10. AI TRADING MEMBERSHIP RULES

KAOS must distinguish between AI market analysis and AI-controlled trading.

## KAOS Free

Free members receive basic strategy-based trading and limited platform capabilities.

AI cannot independently make trading decisions for Free members.

## KAOS Pro

Pro members receive:

- Advanced strategy automation.
- Indicator-based scoring.
- AI market analysis.
- AI confidence estimates.
- Market scanner access.
- Smart opportunity alerts.
- Advanced strategy settings.

**AI is advisory only for Pro.**

The strategy and rule-based scoring engine control trades.

AI cannot independently create, block, or override orders.

## KAOS Ultimate

Ultimate members receive the option to use:

**Strategy Mode:** Rule-based trading decisions.

**AI Mode:** Validated AI signals may authorize trading decisions, subject to independent risk checks.

**Both Mode:** Strategy and AI must agree before a trade can proceed.

AI-controlled execution must remain unavailable until the applicable models and execution safeguards have passed independent validation.

## Official Commission Structure

| Affiliate Membership | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Free | 10% | 0% | 0% |
| Pro | 30% | 15% | 0% |
| Ultimate | 50% | 25% | 5% |

## Commission Rules

- Free affiliates earn commissions one level deep.
- Pro affiliates earn commissions two levels deep.
- Ultimate affiliates earn commissions three levels deep.
- Commission rates depend on each receiving affiliate's membership.
- Commissions do not depend on which membership the purchaser selected.
- Commissions apply to qualifying paid membership purchases and renewals.
- Recruitment alone does not create a cash commission.
- Refunds, chargebacks, downgrades, and cancellations require defined adjustments.
- Payout eligibility and calculation basis must be documented.
- All commissions must be traceable.

The program must be reviewed for MLM, advertising, consumer-protection, and other applicable legal requirements before launch.
