Soft: multiplies by ×1.5 on every loss instead of ×2. Much safer — lower capital exposure. Best for conservative traders.

Anti: increases stake after each WIN, resets on LOSS. Ride winning streaks — only risk your profits, never dig deeper on losses.

Step: only increases after 2 consecutive losses. Single losses reset normally. Filters variance — activates only on real losing streaks.
