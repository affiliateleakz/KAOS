Privacy Policy

KAOS Trading website, Chrome extension, Android and iPhone apps · Updated October 16, 2026

This policy describes how KAOS services handle information when features and integrations are enabled in a released version. Some features remain in development.

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how KAOS Trading ("KAOS", "we", "us") processes information through tradingwithkaos.com, the KAOS Trading Bot Chrome extension, and KAOS Android and iOS apps, where those services are enabled. Public market research feeds may contain information published by third parties. This policy does not replace the privacy policies of brokers or third-party websites.

Information we process

Account and membership: email address, display name, account identifiers, authentication provider identifiers, subscription status, preferences, and account-support communications.

Connected brokers and trading activity: broker-connection identifiers and user-supplied API credentials when you choose to connect a supported broker; account or position information, market selections, orders, trading history, strategy settings, outcomes, and diagnostic snapshots needed for the functions you enable.

Extension and app operation: relevant content or market data from supported trading pages, technical events, browser-extension settings, locally stored candle/history records, device or installation identifiers, error reports, and service access logs (which may include IP address and request metadata). KAOS does not need your unrelated browsing history for its trading features.

Research and AI: watchlists, saved strategies, model inputs and predictions, research requests, feedback, and usage records where those services are enabled.

Payment data: subscription records, payment status, and payment-provider customer identifiers; card payments are handled through Stripe, which processes payment details under its own policy.

How information is used

To authenticate users, verify membership and device access, connect supported brokers when requested, display trading and portfolio information, run selected strategies, maintain records, and deliver alerts or research features users enable.

To maintain security, investigate bugs, recover sessions, validate performance, and improve the accuracy and reliability of the service. Research and AI training features may use market records and trading-outcome data; any cross-user learning or optional sharing needs an appropriately disclosed setting and consent where required.

To send account, billing, security, support, and service notices. Promotional communication, if offered, must include any legally required choices.

Browser extension permissions and page access

The extension runs on listed supported trading domains to display trading interfaces, read relevant market/price and order-result data, and operate user-selected trading functionality. Its permissions may include storage, tabs, scripting, identity, downloads, alarms, and native messaging for disclosed features. It is not intended to monitor unrelated browsing activity.

The extension may keep data in Chrome local storage or communicate with KAOS services at tradingwithkaos.com and api.tradingwithkaos.com. If an optional local research service is installed, the extension may connect to localhost. Exact permissions can change with a reviewed software release.

When data is shared

Service providers and infrastructure that host the KAOS website, databases, authentication, communications, and research processing may process information on our behalf as needed to provide and secure these services.

Supported brokers and exchanges receive account-connection information and order requests only when you enable their integrations. Google may process sign-in data; Stripe processes subscription billing. External AI or research providers may receive relevant requests or data when you explicitly use their connected features.

We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to detect fraud, protect users, or defend the service. Public market, blockchain, and published disclosure information is obtained from its respective sources and subject to their terms.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Extension-derived personal and sensitive user data is used only to provide or improve described user-facing functionality, security, and reliability; it is not sold to data brokers or used for personalized, interest-based, or retargeted advertising. Access by personnel is limited to legitimate support with appropriate consent, security, legal obligations, or de-identified internal operations.

Security and credentials

KAOS is designed to use HTTPS for remote communications and access controls for member information. Broker credentials are sensitive and should be stored on the server using appropriate secret-management and encryption controls rather than embedded in website or app source. Never paste a broker secret into public support requests.

No system can guarantee absolute security. If you suspect unauthorized access, revoke affected broker credentials through the broker and contact support.

Retention, access, and deletion

We retain account, trading, subscription, diagnostic, and security records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the requested services, resolve disputes, meet legal or accounting obligations, and protect the service. Some data may remain temporarily in backups or with independent providers under their retention policies.

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of applicable personal data by emailing support@tradewithkaos.com. Certain transaction or accounting records may have to be retained where required by law. To disconnect an account, also revoke any broker credentials through that broker.

Your choices

You can choose which supported broker to connect, manage available alert and research preferences, disconnect integrations where supported, and stop using or uninstall the browser extension or mobile application. Uninstalling does not automatically delete account records held by KAOS.

Where applicable law provides privacy rights (including California privacy rights), contact us to exercise them. We may need to verify identity before fulfilling a request.

Children and international access

KAOS trading services are intended for adults who are legally eligible to access the relevant markets and services; they are not designed for children. Information may be processed in jurisdictions where KAOS or its service providers operate.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as functionality and data providers change. Material changes will be communicated as required by law. Contact KAOS Trading privacy support at support@tradewithkaos.com.
