Privacy Policy

KAOS Trading website and KAOS Trading Bot Chrome extension · Effective October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information KAOS Trading ("KAOS", "we", "us") collects through tradingwithkaos.com and the KAOS Trading Bot Chrome extension (the "Extension"), why we collect it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create or sign in to a KAOS account we collect your email address, display name and password (stored on our servers only as a one-way hash). If you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your Google account email, name and profile identifier (scopes: openid, email, profile). The Extension stores the resulting KAOS sign-in tokens in Chrome local storage on your device.

Device information. To enforce the number of devices allowed by your membership, the Extension generates a random installation ID and sends it to our servers with the Extension version and your browser's platform name (for example "Win32").

Trading page data (Extension). The Extension runs only on the Pocket Option trading sites listed in its permissions. On those pages it reads the information needed to run the trading tools you start: the selected asset, market prices and candles, your available balance and whether the account is a demo or real account, and the details and results of trades placed by the bot. It does not read pages on other websites, and it does not collect your browsing history.

Trade activity sent to KAOS. When the bot generates a signal or a trade settles, the Extension sends a trade record to our servers so it can appear in your member dashboard: trade ID, asset, direction, stake amount, expiry, strategy name and version, entry and exit price, outcome, profit or loss, timestamp, and the numeric market-feature values and AI prediction used for that trade. Your account balance, broker login and broker session are not included.

KAOS service learning (required). Settled trade features and market outcomes are used to train your personal model, shared KAOS models and the owner's separate research models as a standard part of the service. Training uses a minimized numeric market-feature vector, asset, timeframe, expiration, decision/settlement timing, prices and outcome, with a pseudonymous contributor identifier. Names, emails, broker/account credentials, balances, payment details and raw screenshots are excluded from fitting. A private account link supports access controls, deduplication and deletion; this is de-identified, not irreversible anonymisation. Older data is not silently relabelled as new-policy data. Models remain research-only unless separately validated and authorized for execution.

Payments. Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We receive your subscription status and a Stripe customer ID; we never receive or store your full card number.

Support and logs. We keep messages you send to support and standard server logs (IP address, request time, endpoint and error information) to operate and secure the service.

Optional experimental paper lab. If you explicitly start its public crypto feed, it requests market candles from Binance for the selected symbol. Model files, imported candles and simulated results stay in the paper-test tab unless you export them. The paper lab sends no broker credentials, KAOS account tokens or paper results to that provider, and submits no orders.

Website visitor and referral analytics

On the KAOS website we measure page views, link navigation, referral-link landings and account conversions. We use random first-party browser and visit identifiers, broad page categories, the referring website's hostname, campaign labels and the affiliate code. We do not collect form contents, passwords, full referrer URLs or URL query strings for these reports. Visitor identifiers are pseudonymous browser estimates, not proof of a person's identity.

Our server uses the connection IP transiently to estimate country with a local DB-IP Lite database and to limit abuse. New analytics events do not store the raw IP or send it to a geolocation provider. Country estimates can be wrong, especially with VPNs, mobile networks and proxies; unknown locations remain unknown. Individual analytics events and visit records expire after 90 days. Random browser identifiers are renewed after 90 days, visits after 30 minutes of inactivity, and a referral code is retained for up to 30 days after a referral visit. Anonymous referral click totals may be retained longer. Acquisition source and approximate country attached to an account are retained with that account. Normal security/access logs and payment/referral accounting are separate service records.

Authorized affiliates can see only their own traffic summaries and referred account membership states, without customer email addresses, raw IPs, or other affiliates' traffic. Administrators can see site-wide traffic, sources, countries, affiliate attribution and account membership states. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising. Admin and password-reset pages are excluded from browser analytics.

We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control for optional website analytics. You can also opt out on this browser below. This does not disable account security, payment accounting, or the referral code attached to a membership you choose.

2. Information stored only on your device

The Extension keeps its settings, strategy profiles, recent candle history, a queue of trade records waiting to be sent, and sign-in tokens in Chrome local storage on your computer. If you use the export or backup buttons, the Extension saves files to your Downloads folder. If you install the optional KAOS local research or AI trainer helper, the Extension exchanges market data with it on your own computer (127.0.0.1 or Chrome native messaging); that data does not leave your computer through the helper.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, verify your membership and authorise your devices.
  • To run the trading tools you start and show your trade history and results in the member dashboard.
  • To bill subscriptions and provide customer support.
  • To detect abuse, fix bugs and keep the service reliable.
  • To train and evaluate personal, shared and owner research models using the required, disclosed service-learning pipeline.

4. Why the Extension needs its permissions

  • Access to Pocket Option sites: to read prices and trade results and to run the bot on those pages only.
  • Access to tradingwithkaos.com and api.tradingwithkaos.com: to sign in, check membership and send trade records.
  • storage / unlimitedStorage: to keep settings, candle history and queued trade records on your device.
  • identity: for the optional "Sign in with Google" button.
  • tabs, activeTab, scripting: to find the open trading tab and attach the bot interface to it.
  • alarms: to retry sending queued trade records when the network returns.
  • downloads: to save exports and backups you request.
  • nativeMessaging and localhost access: to talk to the optional KAOS helper installed on your own computer.

5. When we share information

We share information only with service providers that run KAOS for us (web and database hosting, email delivery, Google Sign-In, and Stripe for payments), only as needed to provide the service, and when required by law or to prevent fraud or abuse. We do not sell personal information, share it with data brokers, or use it for personalised or interest-based advertising.

6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from the Extension, including information obtained through Google APIs, adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use it only to provide and improve the Extension's single purpose (KAOS trading tools and the member dashboard); we do not transfer it except as described above; and people at KAOS do not read it except with your permission, for security purposes, to comply with law, or when it has been aggregated and anonymised.

7. Security

Information is sent between the Extension, the website and our servers over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as hashes and access to member data is restricted to authorised staff. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe your account has been compromised, change your password and contact us.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep account, subscription and trade records while your account is active and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless the law requires us to keep billing records longer. Minimized training observations expire after 90 days. Derived model artifacts can remain until retired; deleting your account removes linked training observations and retires models linked to your contribution. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days. You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data by emailing support@tradewithkaos.com; we will respond within 30 days. Uninstalling the Extension deletes the data stored in Chrome on your device but does not delete your KAOS account.

9. Your choices

  • Review model access, training progress and the service-learning policy in the KAOS AI page. Shared service learning is required, not an optional switch.
  • Sign out of the Extension or remove authorised devices from your account.
  • Uninstall the Extension at any time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Contact us to exercise them.

10. Children

KAOS is intended only for adults who are legally allowed to trade in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and notify members of material changes by email or in the Extension. Questions: support@tradewithkaos.com.
